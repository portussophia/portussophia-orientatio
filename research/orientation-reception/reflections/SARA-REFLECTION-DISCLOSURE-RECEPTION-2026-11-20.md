## Consequence

I recommended selective disclosure rather than wholesale publication. The recommendation was not a ranking of research worth. Some records were candidates for release; some might support carefully attributed extracts; others exposed internal operating particulars or prospective architecture warranting restraint.

When the Architect requested a README for future review, the appropriate output changed again. The task became preserving **the decision and its limits**, not publishing the seven originals. The decision record identifies the sources, recommendations, and re-review conditions without copying their bodies.

A public GitHub research branch is still public. Its non-governing label does not make it a confidential archive. **The destination must not decide the source's standing.**
