# Receipts and comments — after orientation

**Status:** Proposed research-preservation separation, not a replacement for the existing Ω lifecycle.

There is a place for receipts and a place for comments **after** orientation. Neither should become a running checklist, substitute for encounter, or premature critique. A receipt records what was actually received at its earned standing; comments may record observations, questions, unresolved distinctions, and costs after sufficient situated understanding.

This area does not create, complete, revise, or supersede PS-RECEIPT-ORIENTATION-001. Any future return must retain its own source, author, date, scope, and standing. No claim of full understanding is made by this scaffold.
