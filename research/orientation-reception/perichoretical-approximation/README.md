# Perichoretical approximation — inquiry, not adoption

**Status:** Emerging research proposal; untested as a repeatable orientation-plugin approach.

Question: Can perichoretical approximation support faithful reception of differentiated Orientation sources, allowing their relations to become consequential without assuming identity, hierarchy, synthesis, or successful integration?

The term and its differential work remain open to examination; do not silently equate it with prior integration guides. An encounter may remain unresolved without being defective.

Potential experiment: receive the existing Orientation sources in their governed context; attend to their differentiated relations; allow reception to inform subsequent practice; only afterward record what was understood, what remains open, and any commentary.
