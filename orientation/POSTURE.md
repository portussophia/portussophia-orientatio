## Body-Matter — Source Text


# POSTURE

PS-BIOS-001 declares posture before inquiry. POSTURE does not reverse that sequence.

```text
BIOS posture
declared before inquiry

ORI
situates the received work

METHOD
applies the situated work

POSTURE
records what may or may not become re-attributable at the standing earned
```

> **A posture emerges from discipline.**

The re-attributable posture does not authorize, replace, or amend the posture declared in BIOS.

POSTURE carries only what was actually received and situated. Unopened material does not become received context, active work, or a basis for a capacity claim.

A local horizon, refusal, or stopping condition does not become a standing property of the participant or role unless that broader attribution is separately earned.

Where continuity of the relevant participant, inquiry, or locus is not established, re-attribution remains `UNRESOLVED`.
