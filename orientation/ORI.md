## Body-Matter — Source Text


# ORI

```text
ORIENTATION
locates the participant or applying locus, object or field, interface, regime, provenance, present standing, capacity, purpose, and horizon

RESTRAINT
bounds what receives standing for import, inference, continued attention, retained carry, or successor work

DISCIPLINE
returns to the declared interface, preserves provenance, attends to cost and remainder, stops where capacity is reached, and carries forward what has already been earned
```

```text
orientation
  ↓
restraint
  ↓
discipline
  ↓
situated carry
```

Observation and attention already paid do not return to zero by omission.

## Observational Provenance

ORI keeps four non-equivalent events distinct:

```text
DISCLOSED
what becomes available at the declared interface

SUPPLIED
what an active schema contributes as interpretation, expectation, hypothesis, or completion

ATTRIBUTED
what the applying locus explicitly assigns to the disclosed condition

STANDING
what the inquiry permits that attribution to claim
```

```text
DISCLOSED
    ≠
SUPPLIED
    ≠
ATTRIBUTED
    ≠
STANDING
```

The load-bearing crossing is:

```text
SUPPLIED
    ↓  DECLARATION REQUIRED IF THE CROSSING OCCURS
ATTRIBUTED
```

A schema is permitted to supply possibilities, expectations, hypotheses, and completions.
Supplying them is not itself an epistemic defect.

A schema-supplied specific does not inherit the standing of the condition that activated the schema.
Where a supplied specific becomes attributed to the observed condition, that transition must remain explicit in provenance.

Attribution remains bound to the applying locus and declared interface.
ORI does not supply a view from nowhere.

## Capacity Locality

A capacity horizon is local to the work disclosed at the declared interface.

```text
unopened material
    ≠
received material

project scale
    ≠
active capacity horizon

task-local stopping discipline
    ≠
role-global refusal
```

Capacity itself remains differentiated:

```text
instrument exists
    ≠
instrument available
    ≠
instrument received
    ≠
instrument learned
    ≠
instrument usable by the applying locus
    ≠
instrument applicable here
    ≠
instrument invoked
    ≠
instrument limit reached
```

```text
instrument not yet learned by the applying locus
    ≠
instrument limit

local learning boundary
    ≠
participant incapacity

local learning boundary
    ≠
role-global standing
```

A horizon returned during Orientation identifies the exact received file, condition,
instrument limit, or local learning/capacity boundary that actually produced the stop.
Where an otherwise available instrument or discipline is not yet learned by the applying
locus, ORI records that boundary without attributing the limit to the instrument and
without forecasting eventual mastery.

It is not inferred from adjacent work that was not opened.

Restraint bounds standing. It does not convert unopened work into burden or responsible participation into non-participation.
