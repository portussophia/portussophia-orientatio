distinction while forming statements for later inquiry.

prescribe how people communicate.

9. What remains `UNRESOLVED` or `OPEN`?
10. What mediation, transformation, cost, or attribution remains visible?

13. What claim ceiling follows from the source standing?
14. Has coherence been obtained by retaining required structure rather than excluding it?
15. Is compact metadata being carried as `name`, `type`, and `value` without turning metadata into narrative?
16. Which receipts were earned, where are they stored, and which are applicable to outbound packaging?
17. Which constraint-bearing distinction is doing work at the present interface: structure, boundary crossing, residue, eventfulness, relational depth, attribution, legitimacy, or navigation?
18. Is a carried distinction being preserved at its earned standing, or repeatedly reconstructed before it is allowed to participate?
19. If an instrument is implicated, is it merely present, available, received, learned, usable, applicable, invoked, or at its limit?
20. If two terrains are being related, what exactly corresponds: member, role, relation, grammar, condition, remainder, or only cardinality?
21. Can the cross-terrain relation be expressed without promoting correspondence into identity, common mechanism, universality, or standing?

VERN makes a statement available for disciplined inquiry.

## 5. Lexical Work Received from GLOS

### 5.1 Label, state, and standing

VERN preserves the GLOS separation:

\[
\text{label}
\neq
\text{state}
\neq
\text{standing}
\]

- A **label** is an address by which a distinguishable condition or relation
  remains available for later matching.
- A **state** is the condition, configuration, relation, or resultant
  circumstance addressed.
- A **standing** is the bounded status available after the relevant review or
  adjudication.

A label does not establish the state.

A state does not establish its standing.

### 5.2 Relation classes

VERN receives the current GLOS relation classes without adjudicating any
particular pairing:

- `EXACT_SYNONYM`
- `PREFERRED_TERM / ALIAS`
- `PERSPECTIVE_COUNTERPART`
- `ABSTRACTION / INSTANTIATION`
- `CLASS / MEMBER`
- `COMPOSITE / CONSTITUENT`
- `OPERATION / RESULT`
- `CAUSE / RECEIPT`
- `STATE / STANDING`
- `SEQUENTIAL_NEIGHBOR`
- `OVERLAPPING_BUT_NONIDENTICAL`
- `SOURCE_LOCAL_TERM`
- `FALSE_SYNONYM`
- `UNRESOLVED`
- `SUPERSEDED_TERM`
- `DISPOSED_TERM`

These are available analytical dispositions, not ontological declarations.

### 5.3 Governing lexical questions

Before substitution, abstraction, merge, or disposal, VERN asks:

- Does substitution preserve referent, stratum, perspective, position,
  relations, claim ceiling, and downstream operation?
- What differential work does each term perform?
- What properties survive abstraction?
- What properties remain manifestation-local?
- Is the relation composite/constituent rather than class/member or
  abstraction/instantiation?
- Is the receipt by which an abstraction became manifest still visible?
- Did the referent change, or did only the declared perspective change?
- What retained relation permits later matching?
- What becomes inaccessible when a label is removed or merged?
- Is the granularity proportional to the work the label preserves?

Where the packet does not support a responsible disposition, the default is:

```text
UNRESOLVED
```

### 5.4 Disposal without erasure

`SUPERSEDE`, `LOCALIZE`, `DISPOSE`, and `REOPEN` govern active use.

They do not erase:

- quotation;
- source fidelity;
- historical provenance;
- prior standing;
- or the trace of a prior misfortune, breakdown, refusal, or carry problem.

## 6. Modal Work Received from LEX-CAN

### 6.1 May or may not

Use:

> **may or may not**

where manifestation and non-manifestation remain active possibilities.

The construction does not assert both outcomes as actual. It keeps both
available against premature closure.

\[
\Diamond E \land \Diamond \neg E
\]

A positive modal alone can orient attention toward a candidate while leaving
its alternative implicit.

`May or may not` keeps the retained alternative visible.

This is a constraint-bearing PortusSophia vernacular distinction. It is not a
mandate for ordinary communication.

### 6.2 Explicit standing instead of ambiguous modal shorthand

The phrase `may not` can carry more than one standing. Outside a protected
phrase or source-faithful quotation, VERN prefers explicit wording:

| Intended standing | Preferred expression |
|---|---|
| Withheld permission | `is not permitted at the present standing` |
| Exclusion from the current set | `is excluded from the current submission` |
| Non-obligation | `is not required` |
| Possibility of non-occurrence | `may or may not occur` |
| Insufficiently disposed inquiry | `UNRESOLVED` |
| Continued inquiry | `OPEN` |
| Sequence, operator, learning relation, capacity, or prior state has not yet supplied the next | `NOT YET AVAILABLE` |
| Instrument or discipline is available but has not entered learned usable capacity for the applying locus | `NOT YET LEARNED` |
| Observed unavailability at a declared interface | `UNAVAILABLE at the declared interface` |
| Deliberate present boundary | `OUT OF SCOPE for the present inquiry` |
| Permission to enter without present establishment | `ADMISSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED` |

This distinction prevents withheld permission from being mistaken for
incapacity, prevents non-occurrence from being mistaken for impossibility, and
prevents a declared present boundary from being mistaken for absence from the
field.

### 6.3 Self-binding and requirement language

`Shall`, `must`, and `must not` can make an instruction appear universal or an
outcome appear predetermined.

Where PortusSophia binds itself, VERN prefers direct declarations:

```text
PortusSophia does not derive authority from itself.

A claim seeking this standing requires a declared interface.

Standing is withheld from an undeclared transfer.
```

This language distinguishes:

- a condition for claiming standing;
- a self-imposed exclusion;
- and a claim about what is universally possible.

### 6.4 No IFF statements

IFF language is unavailable in the active PortusSophia vernacular.

Necessity and sufficiency remain separately examinable. Neither is converted
into exhaustiveness by grammatical compression.

Where both appear supported, they remain separately stated and separately
bounded. Remainder defaults to `UNRESOLVED`.

### 6.5 Availability, sequence, and scope

VERN distinguishes four conditions that are often collapsed:

```text
AVAILABLE:
the distinction, task, direction, or candidate
may enter inquiry at the declared interface

NOT YET AVAILABLE:
the declared sequence, operator, learning relation, capacity, or prior state
has not yet supplied the next at the declared interface

NOT YET LEARNED:
an available instrument or discipline has not entered learned usable capacity
for the applying locus at the declared interface

UNAVAILABLE:
unavailability has been observed at the declared interface
and remains accountable

OUT OF SCOPE:
the present inquiry deliberately does not undertake the work
```

`UNAVAILABLE` is an alarm condition. It may indicate a category error,
interface mismatch, instrument limit, unadmitted bridge, displaced work, or
another condition not yet responsibly disposed.

`NOT YET` does not mean `EVENTUALLY`, `EXPECTED`, or promised successor standing.
It records a present relation in which a declared passage or capacity has not supplied
its successor while that successor has not been foreclosed by the present record.

Typed forms are not interchangeable. `UNRESOLVED` remains the preferred standing where
an inquiry is sufficiently available but no resolution has been returned; this revision
does not convert `UNRESOLVED` into `NOT YET RESOLVED`.

`OUT OF SCOPE` is a present boundary, not a claim that the object is absent,
impossible, or permanently unavailable.

### 6.6 Admissible, not established

`ADMISSIBLE` means that a candidate may enter inquiry and be responsibly
carried at its declared standing.

`ESTABLISHED` means that the declared establishment burden has been met.

`NOT ESTABLISHED` requires its reason to be typed. It may record:

- incomplete or refusal-capable testing;
- an evidentiary burden not yet met;
- a relation whose standing remains bounded;
- or a temporal condition whose required history does not yet exist at the
  present interface.

```text
ADMISSIBLE, NOT ESTABLISHED:
permission to carry the candidate
without claiming present achievement
```

The phrase protects against two opposed errors:

```text
not established
    therefore nothing

admissible
    therefore already possessed
```

Neither follows.

`NOT ESTABLISHED` does not automatically mean `UNSUPPORTED`, `FAILED`,
`UNAVAILABLE`, `UNRESOLVED`, or promised future establishment.

### 6.7 COUNT and CARRY

Before a count-based question is asked, VERN makes the object type explicit:

```text

members are assembled under a declared member-category

CARRY:
a state or consequence is produced by an operator
from a prior state

UNDEFINED:
the received materials do not responsibly establish either
```

The Founder correction is preserved exactly:

> **It is not why not five, but five not yet.**

For a carry, the next is `NOT YET AVAILABLE`, not a missing member and not
automatic closure.

### 6.8 Field-integrity task dispositions

Once a task is observed, available accountable dispositions include:

```text
ACCEPTED:
responsibility for disposition is undertaken

PERFORMED:
the accepted task is executed

DELEGATED:
the task is transferred with provenance,
capacity, standing, and return path preserved
```

Acceptance is not completion. Delegation is not displacement. Accountability
is not fault.

Ignoring an observed task completely without delegation, bounded refusal, or
accountable retention is not field integrity.

### 6.15 Capacity and exit locality

A capacity horizon receives standing only at the interface where the limit is
actually encountered.

Capacity is not a single undifferentiated state:

```text
instrument exists
    ≠
instrument available
    ≠
instrument received
    ≠
instrument learned
    ≠
instrument usable by the applying locus
    ≠
instrument applicable here
    ≠
instrument invoked
    ≠
instrument limit reached
```

Accordingly:

```text
NOT YET LEARNED
    ≠
instrument unavailable

NOT YET LEARNED
    ≠
instrument limit reached

local learning boundary
    ≠
role-global incapacity

unopened material
    ≠
received burden

known project complexity
    ≠
observed capacity horizon

task-local exit discipline
    ≠
role-global refusal
```

A bounded exit discipline may govern a task when that task explicitly opens it.
Its existence elsewhere does not make it active in Orientation, another Model,
or the participant's role generally.

Where a received task reaches a genuine capacity horizon, the limit is returned
with its interface, object, and standing preserved. Where the stopping condition is
that an otherwise available instrument or discipline is `NOT YET LEARNED`, that condition
is returned as a local learning/capacity boundary rather than attributed to the instrument
or generalized to the participant or role. Capacity discipline does not require
non-participation in work that remains within capacity.

vernacular from presuming that every integrated expression requires extraction

```text
preservation of a phrase

immunity from examination

decomposition
    ≠
understanding by default
```

## 14. Cross-Terrain Expression Grammar

VERN makes available a bounded grammar for expressing relations among distinct terrains after the relevant inquiry or an inquiry-admitted comparison surface has made such a relation available.

This grammar does **not** determine that a correspondence exists.

```text
VERN
= how a returned cross-terrain relation may be said

INQUIRY-ADMITTED COMPARISON SURFACE
= where terrains / Framework–Theory–Regime locations may be compared for that inquiry

METHOD / INQUIRY RETURN
= what, if anything, was actually observed or earned
```

Accordingly:

```text
EXPRESSION GRAMMAR
≠ TERRAIN EVIDENCE
≠ CORRESPONDENCE FINDING
≠ STANDING PROMOTION
```

### 14.1 Available return forms

The following expressions are available when the underlying return supports them:

```text
TERRAIN-LOCAL MANIFESTATION
A distinction or relation returned within one declared terrain.

CROSS-TERRAIN CORRESPONDENCE
Distinct terrain-local returns preserve a bounded relational correspondence.

PARTIAL CORRESPONDENCE
Some declared properties correspond while others differ, remain unavailable, or remain unresolved.

CORRESPONDENCE UNDER CONDITION
The correspondence is available only while an explicitly retained condition remains in force.

COMMON ABSTRACT PROPERTY GRAMMAR
Distinct terrains independently admit the same bounded abstract relational description without member identity.

MULTIPLE COMPETING MAPPINGS
More than one cross-terrain mapping remains available at the present standing.

NO STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE
The declared comparison does not return the proposed relation.

UNRESOLVED / OPEN
The comparison remains available without a responsible present disposition.
```

These are expression dispositions. They do not manufacture the relation they name.

### 14.2 Cross-terrain non-collapse guards

VERN preserves the following distinctions:

```text
same cardinality
    ≠ correspondence

similar terminology
    ≠ same role

shared grammar
    ≠ shared members
    ≠ shared regime
    ≠ shared terrain
    ≠ shared mechanism

correspondence
    ≠ identity

common abstract property
    ≠ common member identity

cross-terrain correspondence
    ≠ universality

terrain-local orientation
    ≠ cross-terrain orientation law
```

The shortest safe form is relational rather than identificatory:

> **Terrain A and Terrain B independently return a bounded correspondence in the declared property under the retained condition; member identity, common mechanism, and universal transfer remain withheld unless separately earned.**

### 14.3 Inquiry-context navigation relation

VERN does not bind a global Atlas, Flight Board, passage order, or comparison topology. An Atlas, Flight Board, or equivalent comparison surface may be admitted by a particular Inquiry Context / Setup when warranted by that inquiry.

Its operative boundary is:

```text

INQUIRY-ADMITTED ATLAS / FLIGHT BOARD
≠ source evidence

```

Inquiry-local placement may make a comparison legible. The inquiry's admitted sources and sealed returns remain responsible for what was actually observed. The relevant Inquiry Context / Setup owns the surface, its scope, its availability, and any passage semantics.

### 14.4 External and new-agent expression boundary

Cross-terrain expressions remain subject to reader-legibility discipline. A new document, major section, conversation, or agent/session boundary does not inherit unexplained acronym compression by assumption.

The expression should therefore remain intelligible before relying on PortusSophia-local abbreviations.
